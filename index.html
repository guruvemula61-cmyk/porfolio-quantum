The portfolio selection problem

Choose a combination of assets that balances expected return, risk, and diversification — inside a fixed budget and a limit on how many assets can be held.

Expected Return ↑

Maximize the weighted return across the chosen assets.

Risk ↓

Minimize exposure using the asset covariance structure.

Diversification ↑

Avoid concentrating the portfolio in correlated assets.

Constraints

A fixed budget and a maximum number of selected assets.

Why QAOA?

Portfolio selection involves searching through many possible asset combinations while satisfying multiple constraints. We formulate the selection problem as a QUBO and use QAOA to explore candidate solutions through a hybrid quantum-classical optimization workflow.
